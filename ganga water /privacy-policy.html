Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ganga Water Tank Cleaning Services collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website (gangawatertankcleaning.com) or communicate with us to enquire about our services. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information primarily to respond to your service requests and improve our website experience. The types of data we may collect include:

  • Form Submissions: Information you provide when submitting enquiries, such as your name, phone number, service required, and any specific messages.
  • WhatsApp Communications: Data exchanged when you contact us via WhatsApp links on our website.
  • Website Cookies: Standard analytics data, such as page views and interactions, to understand how visitors use our site.
  • Device/Browser Info: Basic, non-personally identifiable technical information (e.g., browser type, operating system) collected for analytics and security.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used in the following ways:

  • Responding to Enquiries: To reply to your quotes, queries, and service requests.
  • Service Delivery: To arrange and schedule tank cleaning services at your requested location.
  • Business Communication: To follow up regarding bookings, provide estimates, and ensure customer satisfaction.

4. WhatsApp Communications

Our website utilizes WhatsApp as a primary contact method. Please note that clicking the "WhatsApp Us" buttons or submitting forms that redirect to WhatsApp will open the WhatsApp application on your device. Data transmitted via this method is processed and governed by WhatsApp's platform and its respective privacy policies.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze website traffic and improve user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect certain functionalities of the website.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain integrations or links to third-party services, including:

  • Google Maps (for location display and routing)
  • WhatsApp (for communication)
  • Cloudinary & Unsplash (for image hosting and placeholder graphics)

These third-party services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle your data.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please be aware that no transmission of data over the internet can be reliable as 100% secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and communication data for as long as necessary to fulfill the service purposes outlined in this policy, or to comply with our legal and business obligations.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of the data we hold about you. Please contact us to exercise these rights.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. Your continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
